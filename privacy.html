Janet privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Janet is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It has no other users.

What Janet reads

With the owner's permission, Janet reads the owner's Gmail using Google's read-only permission (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, change or delete email.

How it is used

Email is read and summarized on the owner's computer, by an AI model that runs on that computer, to prepare the owner's morning briefing. The briefing is sent only to the owner's own private Telegram chat.

Email content is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, not sent to any cloud AI service, and not used to train AI models.

Janet's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and removal

The sign-in and the briefing notes are kept only on the owner's computer. Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
